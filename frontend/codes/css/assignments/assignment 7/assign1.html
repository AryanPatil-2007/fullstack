Popular Destinations

Goa

Goa is famous for its beautiful beaches, amazing sunsets, and delicious local food.

PLACES TO VISIT

  • Baga Beach
  • Fort Aguada
  • Dudhsagar Falls

Manali

Manali offers snow-covered mountains, peacefull valleys, and exciting adventure activities.

PLACES TO VISIT

  • Solang Valley
  • Rohtang Pass
  • Hadimba Temple

Jaipur

Jaipur is know for its royal palaces, historic forts, and colorful traditional markets.

PLACES TO VISIT

  • Hawa Mahal
  • Amber Fort
  • City Palace

Travel Tips

Always carry your identification documents.
